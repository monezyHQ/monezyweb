What to include

To help us respond, include:

  • Your name and the email we should reply to.
  • Whether the issue is about the Android app, the website, or a privacy request.
  • For app issues: device model, Android version, and app version from Play Store or Settings.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.

We aim to reply to genuine user mail within a few business days.
